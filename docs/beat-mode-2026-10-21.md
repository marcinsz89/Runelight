## State and timing

`SoundMode` is saved in `runelight_controls` as `soundMode`; an existing Boolean
`soundEnabled` is read only as migration fallback and removed on the next save.
Explicit mode values win. The service receives an explicit mode parameter and
publishes it with sound runtime state. The compatibility Boolean extra remains
for existing internal tests/callers, mapping to Off/On.

On/Beat changes update the existing `AudioEnvelope` through the live recorder;
they do not replace the AudioRecord, projection, video lease or effect. Changing
response resets old envelope/filter state. Off releases audio and restores the
base effect. Stale callbacks, permission handling, Stop and unsupported-effect
pause retain the existing ownership rules.

## Signal processing

- On keeps the original RMS mapping: floor 0.003, gain 6, 35ms attack/180ms release.
- Beat measures broad energy plus a bass-weighted signal, using the difference
  between 250Hz and 40Hz low-pass filters at the existing 16kHz sample rate.
- Compare energy with the previous background estimate: gate threshold is the
  larger of 0.015 and 1.35 × background. Background follows sampled energy with
  a 700ms time constant. This lets steady content settle below the gate.
- Scale excess energy against the larger of 0.07 and 1.25 × background, clamp to
  0–1, and apply exponent 1.4 to reduce weak responses. Attack is 10ms, release
  70ms; convergence still snaps to exact zero. Initial background is seeded from
  the first block, so a hit immediately at startup may be suppressed.
- No FFT, tempo predictor, lookahead, extra read buffer or new processing thread.
  The existing nonblocking 25ms poll and paced latest-frame vendor writer remain.

This is a deliberately small transient-energy response, not musical source
separation. Loud melodic changes can trigger it; compressed music with weak
transients may respond less. Real music/volume tuning is still acceptance work.

Concept references: [noise gate attack/decay](https://manual.audacityteam.org/man/noise_gate.html)
and [onset threshold/silence controls](https://aubio.org/doc/old/onset_8h.html).
No comparator code or DSP dependency was copied.
